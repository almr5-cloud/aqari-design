📖 How to use Aqari Design

Build a professional listing post in under a minute:

1
Fill in the listing details
Type the headline, location, price, beds, baths and square footage in the left panel. Everything updates live.
2
Upload the property photo
Click or drag a photo in. Use the zoom slider and drag inside the preview to frame it exactly how you want.
3
Pick a template, size and color
Six templates built for listings, three sizes (feed, portrait and story), and your own brand color.
4
Add your brokerage details
Your name, phone, logo, brokerage name and license number — plus the Equal Housing Opportunity mark when you need it.
5
Download and post
Hit Download PNG or JPG. The file exports at full resolution, ready for Instagram or Facebook.

To cancel: sign in to whop.com with the same email, open Orders, choose Aqari Design and hit Cancel. Your access stays active until the end of the month you paid for.
